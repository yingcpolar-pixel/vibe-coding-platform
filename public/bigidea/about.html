Why C-POLAR

Most pollutants carry a negative charge.

So we engineered a permanent positive one.

Viruses, bacteria, pollen, fungi, PFOAs, and wildfire smoke are almost all negatively charged. Conventional filters trap them mechanically — then clog, weaken, and can release what they caught. NanoFlashing™ takes a different path: a mechanical, permanently positive-charged instrument that captures negatively charged pollutants and destroys living threats by physical action. The charge does the work, and the material never runs out.
