Why it outlasts conventional filtration

Filters fade. A built-in charge doesn't.

A HEPA filter can lose capture as it loads, shed trapped spores back into the air, and add pressure, energy, and hardware to every system it sits in. NanoFlashing™ works by electrostatic capture and physical destruction — the charge is the source, and the material carries it permanently. Less maintenance, fewer replacements, and protection that doesn't quietly degrade between service intervals.
