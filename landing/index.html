The problem

Most pollutants carry a negative charge.

RED = electron-rich (negative). Standard electrostatic-potential convention. An anion has more electrons than protons — a net negative charge. Those extra electrons crowd onto the most electronegative atoms: oxygen, fluorine, sulfur. And a net negative charge is exactly what a positive surface pulls in.
